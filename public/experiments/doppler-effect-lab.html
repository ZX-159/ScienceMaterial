v = f·λ. In air ~340 m/s, water ~1500 m/s. Doppler shift depends on vs/v ratio, not absolute v.
Observer
Front observer: f' = f·v/(v−vs) > f (higher pitch). Behind: f' = f·v/(v+vs) < f (lower).
f' = f · v / (v ∓ vs) — moving source
f' = f · (v ± vo)/v — moving observer
Combined: f' = f·(v±vo)/(v∓vs)
Light: redshift z = (λ_obs−λ_emit)/λ_emit. Hubble: distant galaxies redshift → universe expanding.
